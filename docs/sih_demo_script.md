# SMRITIVAN: SIH 2026 Demonstration Script

**Team:** laccha paratha  
**Problem Statement:** 26003 (AI-Based Cognitive Gaming and Memory Assistance Platform for Elderly Dementia Patients in NER)  

## Preparation
1. Ensure the device has **no internet connection** (Airplane mode) to prove the offline-first capability.
2. Have the FastAPI backend running locally but disconnected from the phone.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 - The Problem & The Welcome
*   **Action:** Launch the Smritivan App.
*   **Speaker:** "Judges, the North Eastern Region faces a rising challenge with elderly dementia, compounded by remote geography and poor internet. Meet SMRITIVAN: a calm, offline-first memory garden."
*   **Visual:** Show the Splash Screen transitioning to the calm, Sage Green Welcome screen.

## ⏱️ Minute 0:30 - Accessibility & Cultural Personalization
*   **Action:** Navigate through Onboarding. Select **Assamese** as the language, and **Assam** as the region. 
*   **Speaker:** "Notice our UI. There are no overwhelming dashboards. We ask for language and region first. Instantly, the app adapts to Assamese text and voice, ensuring zero digital literacy barriers for our elderly users."

## ⏱️ Minute 1:15 - AI-Driven Cognitive Engagement (Offline)
*   **Action:** Enter 'Elder Mode' and start the 'Visual Memory Game'.
*   **Speaker:** "We are entirely offline right now. SMRITIVAN serves culturally familiar imagery—like Muga Silk and Bihu patterns—rather than generic hospital tests. It feels like home."
*   **Action:** Play one round poorly (intentional errors/delay).
*   **Speaker:** "Behind the scenes, our on-device AI Engine analyzes latency and error rates. Because I struggled, the next round dynamically decreases in difficulty, preventing anxiety."

## ⏱️ Minute 2:30 - The Caregiver Loop
*   **Action:** Turn on WiFi. Open Caregiver Mode via PIN auth.
*   **Speaker:** "SMRITIVAN isn't just for the elderly. Caregivers use a secure PIN to access a non-clinical dashboard."
*   **Visual:** Show Caregiver Dashboard with the Cognitive Vitality Score (CVS) and missed Reminders.
*   **Speaker:** "The moment the internet reconnects, our background Sync Queue pushes the cached AI telemetry to our secure PostgreSQL backend, allowing doctors miles away to monitor disease progression without the patient ever leaving their village."

## ⏱️ Minute 3:00 - Closing
*   **Speaker:** "Accessible, deeply localized, completely functional offline, and powered by AI. Team laccha paratha presents SMRITIVAN—restoring memory, routine, and dignity to the North East."
